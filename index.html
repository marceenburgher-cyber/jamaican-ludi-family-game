About Jamaican Ludi

Ludi is a family-friendly Jamaican game that brings generations together through strategy, friendly competition, laughter, and lively table talk.

Ludi belongs to the traditional Ludo family of games played in different forms around the world. No exclusive ownership is claimed over the underlying traditional method of play.
